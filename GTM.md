# GTM.md (Go-To-Market Strategy)

## The Exact Target User
Our "Bullseye" customer is the **Engineering Manager (EM) or Head of Operations** at a seed-to-Series A startup (10–50 employees) that has recently raised capital. They are currently in "growth at all costs" mode and have likely allowed team members to expense 4–5 different AI tools (Cursor, Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT) simultaneously without any centralized oversight.

## What they Google / Scroll right before wanting this
- "Cursor vs Copilot for teams"
- "How to manage startup AI tool sprawl"
- "Reducing AWS/OpenAI costs"
- Scrolling through **"The Rundown AI"** newsletter or **"Ben's Bites"** on X.

## Distribution Channels
- **Specific Subreddits**: r/startups, r/SaaS (specifically answering tool-fatigue threads).
- **Slack/Discord Groups**: "Latent Space" Discord, "YC Founders" Slack (Internal channels).
- **The "Viral Loop"**: The Results page includes a "Compare with Industry Benchmark" button that encourages users to share their "Clean Bill of Health" or "Shocking Overspend" results on X/LinkedIn.

## First 100 Users Strategy ($0 Budget)
1. **The "Audit-First" Outreach**: Cold DM 50 founders on X/LinkedIn with a personalized observation: "Hey [Name], noticed your team is hiring for [Role]—likely scaling your AI stack. We built a free auditor to see if you're overpaying for seats. Most Series A teams save $800/mo."
2. **Product Hunt Ship**: Launch as a "Free Tool for Founders."
3. **Founder Communities**: High-value poaching in founder-specific WhatsApp groups by offering a "Stack Health Check."

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
**Credex's existing inventory**. We can leverage our access to discounted credits to offer a "Switch and Save" guarantee: "If our auditor finds savings, we'll give you your first month of Credex-managed Cursor/Claude for free."

## Week-1 Traction Goals
- 500 visitors
- 150 audits completed (30% conversion)
- 45 leads captured (30% conversion from results)
- 5 consultation calls booked for high-value (> $1,000/mo) savings.
