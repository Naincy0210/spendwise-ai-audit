# USER_INTERVIEWS.md

## Interview 1: Sarah T. - Founder, Stealth AI Agent Startup
**Role:** Founder & CEO (formerly EM at Stripe)
**Company Stage:** Seed (7 employees)

**Direct Quotes:**
1. "I don't even know what my team has signed up for. I just see 'Anthropic', 'OpenAI', and 'Cursor' appearing on the corporate Card."
2. "We definitely have seat overlap. Half the team uses Copilot, the other half uses Cursor. We're paying for both."
3. "I would kill for a tool that tells me which seat is actually 'active' vs just sitting there costing $20/mo."

**The most surprising thing they said:**
She mentioned that she sometimes uses her personal Plus account for work because the company Team plan is "too slow" for her specific timezone.

**What it changed about your design:**
Introduced the "Usage Type" (Primary/Secondary) for tools to identify where teams are paying for secondary tools that provide redundant value.
