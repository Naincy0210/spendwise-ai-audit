# ECONOMICS.md

## Lead Value to Credex
A converted lead (a startup that switches their AI spend to Credex-managed credits) is worth approximately **$2,400 ARR (net margin)**.
- **Reasoning**: Average startup spends $2,000–$5,000/mo on AI infrastructure. Credex takes a 5–10% management fee or achieves a buy-low/sell-high spread. $3,000/mo * 12 months * 7% margin = $2,520.

## Conversion Funnel Math
To be profitable:
1. **Audit Completed** -> 100%
2. **Lead Captured (Email)** -> 25%
3. **Consultation Booked** -> 5%
4. **Credit Purchase** -> 2% (of original audits)

**Math**: 100 audits -> 2 purchases.
2 * $2,400 = $4,800 Revenue.
Cost of 100 audits (infra + AI) = < $50.
**ROI = 96x**. Even with a 0.5% conversion rate, the tool is highly profitable as a dedicated top-of-funnel asset.

## Driving $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR ($83k/mo margin):
- We need ~400 active monthly customers (at $200 margin/mo).
- To get 400 customers at a 2% funnel conversion, we need **20,000 audits completed**.
- **The True Requirement**: We need a viral distribution engine (referral codes + social sharing) that drives ~1,100 audits per month. This is achievable via partnerships with accelerators (Y Combinator, Techstars) who provide SpendWise as a "Day 1" resource for their batches.
